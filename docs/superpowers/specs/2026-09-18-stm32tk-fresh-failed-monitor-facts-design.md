## Problem and user scenarios

VS10-B must diagnose its current failed firmware before authorizing a fix. The existing `physical-monitor-fact/1` selector is for historical A supplementary assessment: `diagnostics/model.py:280-298` requires `continuation_evidence_id`, and `diagnostic_workflows.py:3048` authenticates a completed before/after continuation pair. B explicitly has no continuation. The installed pure parser accepts a retained valid v1 step but rejects the same shape without that field as `DIAGNOSTIC_PLAN_INVALID`; evidence is `evidence/diagnostic-entry-preflight/selector-parser-check.json`. No B fault has been flashed and no timed B attempt has begun.

Three runnable scenarios define the change:

1. A Diagnostic bound to a real, current failed physical Target run evaluates its already-published failed-before Monitor window: `testtime` variation and PE3/PE4 bit-value sets. The original evidence is authenticated and recomputed before plan execution, assessment and event replay; a repair or continuation proof is not required beforehand.
2. Existing A historical v1 selectors retain their exact serialized bytes, continuation requirement, accepted evidence and rejection behavior.
3. A selector naming another run/session/project/build/probe, a fixed-after window, replay evidence, missing/uncommitted roots, changed transcript/ref bytes or insufficient samples is rejected with existing input/identity/integrity/environment errors.

Non-goals: new hardware controls, new Monitor acquisition/publication/analysis algorithms, B continuation, cross-session projection, fresh fixed-after selectors, arbitrary evidence selectors, lower quality thresholds, changes to FixVerification, and repeated normal firmware/IDE acceptance.
